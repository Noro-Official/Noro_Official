# Noro

**Noro** is an open foundation for building clarity in the age of complex systems.

We develop tools, curricula, and frameworks that make deep technical learning accessible, resilient, and conceptually beautiful—starting with quantum computing and quantum error correction.

## Why Noro?

Most modern sciences teach *what* to compute before they teach *how* to think.

Noro flips that.  
We start with structure, feedback, and correction.  
From quantum bits to first principles, we believe clarity should propagate.

## What We Build

Noro supports educational systems that emphasize:

- **Structure over memorization**  
- **Intuition grounded in feedback and correction**  
- **Open, modular design** for labs, classrooms, and self-learners

Our projects aim to feel like the Arduino of post-classical education: hands-on, powerful, and beautifully simple.

### Featured Project

🌱 **[OpenQStack](https://github.com/noro-org/openqstack)**  
A minimal, elegant platform for teaching quantum error correction, feedback cycles, and decoding logic. It’s clean, transparent, and runs anywhere Python does.

## Our Mission

> To stabilize complex learning by building open systems for structured thought.

Noro exists to lower the noise floor in modern education.

We empower anyone—regardless of institution—to work directly with the fundamental logic behind systems like quantum error correction, classical codes, and beyond.
